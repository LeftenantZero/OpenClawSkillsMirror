## Description:

Nano Banana 2 image generation and editing skill for AI Hive that helps agents create, edit, combine reference images, preserve character consistency, and produce product or marketing visuals.

## Use Case:

External creators, marketers, ecommerce operators, and developers use this skill to generate or edit images with Nano Banana 2 through AI Hive. It supports text-to-image, image editing, multi-reference composition, character consistency, product imagery, posters, and marketing asset workflows.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Prompts and selected reference images are sent to AI Hive for generation or editing.

Mitigation: Use only media and prompts that are appropriate to share with AI Hive, and avoid submitting confidential or restricted material unless approved for that service.

Risk: AI Hive API credentials may be stored in a local config file.

Mitigation: Prefer AI_HIVE_API_KEY when avoiding local credential storage, and keep any config file permissions restricted.

Risk: Generated visuals can contain inaccurate text, product details, legal claims, or other commercial content.

Mitigation: Manually review required text, numbers, product facts, prices, and legal information before publishing or using generated assets commercially.
