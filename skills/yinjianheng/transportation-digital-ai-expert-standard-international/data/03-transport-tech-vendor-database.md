## How to Read This Database

**T-MRL Level (Transport Maturity Readiness Level, L1–L5)** — how mature/production-ready the vendor's transport-technology offering is:

- **L1** — Research concept / early-stage
- **L2** — Prototype / pilot deployment
- **L3** — Validated / early commercial availability
- **L4** — Commercial deployment at scale
- **L5** — Mature, dominant market leader

**Tech-Autonomy Rating (1–5)** — the degree of autonomous operation the technology delivers:

- **1** — Manual / human-operated
- **2** — Decision-support for operators
- **3** — Assisted / semi-automated
- **4** — High automation (limited human oversight)
- **5** — Fully autonomous (no human in the loop)
