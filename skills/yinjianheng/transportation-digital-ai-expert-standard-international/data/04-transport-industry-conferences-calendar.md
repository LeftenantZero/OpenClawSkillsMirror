# Global Transport & ITS Industry Conference Calendar

A curated reference of major global transportation, intelligent transport systems (ITS), and mobility conferences. This calendar spans road, rail, aviation, maritime, logistics, traffic safety, autonomous mobility, smart cities, digital twin, energy/sustainability, and regional events. Last updated: July 2026.

Conferences are grouped by domain. Months indicate the **typical** timing; actual dates vary by year. Always verify on the official website before planning travel.

## Notes

- **Frequency**: Many conferences are annual; some are biennial (every 2 years) or triennial. Check the website for the current cycle.
- **Rotation**: "Rotating" means the host city changes each edition. Most major global congresses rotate across continents.
- **Virtual / Hybrid**: Post-2020, many events offer virtual or hybrid participation options. Verify with the organizer.
- **Call for Papers (CFP)**: Deadlines are typically 6–9 months before the event. Plan submissions accordingly.
- **Exhibitions**: Large events (InnoTrans, Intertraffic, SMM, Passenger Terminal EXPO) include major trade exhibitions alongside the conference program.
- **Accuracy**: While every effort has been made to list real conferences, dates, locations, and websites may change. Always confirm directly with the organizer's official website.
