## Description: <br>
财报分析入门工具 helps personal investors and junior analysts extract financial data from local Excel or PDF files and generate interactive HTML reports with sparkline trend charts. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[thcjp](https://clawhub.ai/user/thcjp) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users such as personal investors and junior analysts use this skill to turn a single local Excel or PDF financial report into an HTML analysis report with basic metrics, trend visuals, and forecast markers. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Sensitive financial documents may be read into an AI-assisted workflow. <br>
Mitigation: Review agent and provider privacy settings before processing confidential Excel or PDF files, and use the skill only for financial-report analysis. <br>
Risk: Generated metrics, trend charts, or simple forecasts may be incomplete or misleading if the input data is incomplete or poorly structured. <br>
Mitigation: Check the source report quality, prefer structured Excel inputs when available, and review generated analysis before relying on it. <br>
